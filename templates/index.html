Prerequisite Knowledge Graph

Concepts are mapped as a prerequisite DAG with 70% mastery gating. Dependent concepts remain locked until demonstrated understanding is verified.

Real Scikit-Learn Engine

A Decision Tree classifier analyzes 8 educational signals (scores, attempts, trend slope, engagement) to classify learning state: Improving, Stable, or Needs Support.

AI Contextual Re-Theming

Mathematical and computational problems are re-themed into the student's personal interests (Space, Robotics, Gaming) with strict mathematical invariance guardrails.
